How can we assist you today?

SK Fortis Healthcare is one of India's leading integrated healthcare delivery service providers. The healthcare verticals of the company primarily comprise hospitals, diagnostics, and day care specialty facilities with over 28 healthcare facilities, 4,500+ operational beds, and a team of globally acclaimed clinicians.

We provide comprehensive patient services including 24x7 Emergency Trauma & Ambulance assistance, OPD Consultations, In-patient admissions, Critical Care (ICU/CCU/NICU), Robotic Surgery, Advanced Radiotherapy, Teleconsultations, and dedicated International Patient Services desks with multilingual translation support.

All hospitals under our network operate in strict compliance with NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers), JCI (Joint Commission International), NABL accredited laboratories, AERB radiation safety guidelines, and clinical biomedical waste management protocols.

Our clinical outcomes are audited quarterly and benchmarked against top global medical institutions: >99.2% surgical success rate in adult cardiac bypass surgeries, >92% 5-year survival in liver transplants, and zero-infection protocols in modular ultra-clean laminar flow operating theaters.
