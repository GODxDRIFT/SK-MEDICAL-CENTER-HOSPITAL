Ayushman PM-JAY & Cashless Insurance

How to Claim Cashless Treatment Under Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) & Private TPAs at S.K Hospital

Medical Review: Hospital TPA & Insurance Desk
Published: 2026 5 min read

Insurance & Welfare Advisory

Provided by the Patient Welfare & TPA Desk at S.K Multi-Speciality Hospital to streamline hassle-free cashless hospitalization for patients and families.

1. Cashless Healthcare at S.K Multi-Speciality Hospital

Medical emergencies and planned surgeries shouldn't burden families with sudden financial stress. At S.K Multi-Speciality Hospital, our dedicated 24x7 TPA desk assists patients in utilizing their government schemes like Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY), CGHS/ECHS references, and major corporate health insurance policies.

2. Documents Required for Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) Admission

  • Ayushman Golden Card (PM-JAY Card) with active QR code/ABHA ID.
  • Original Aadhaar Card of the patient for biometric/OTP authentication.
  • Ration Card or Voter ID for family verification.
  • Previous Doctor Prescription / Referral Slip (if available).

3. Private Insurance & TPA Cashless Claim Process

  1. Present Health Card: Submit your digital/physical health insurance e-card and Aadhaar card at our TPA desk.
  2. Pre-Authorization Form: Our medical coordinator and attending physician fill out the clinical pre-auth documentation within 1 hour.
  3. Insurer Approval: The TPA grants initial cashless authorization sanctioning inpatient admission and treatment.
  4. Zero Out-of-Pocket Stress: You only pay for non-medical consumables while the hospital settles the main hospital bill directly with the insurer upon discharge.

Need Help with Insurance Policy or Ayushman Card?

Visit our ground floor TPA desk or call our insurance coordinator for free eligibility check.
