Intensive Care Unit (ICU & HDU)

24×7 Critical Care, Ventilator & HDU Beds

Our Intensive Care Unit is designed for critically ill patients requiring continuous medical intervention, cardiac stabilization, post-operative monitoring, and mechanical ventilation.

  • Advanced invasive & non-invasive mechanical ventilators
  • Multi-parameter vital monitors (ECG, SpO2, NIBP, EtCO2)
  • Central continuous oxygen & medical suction pipelines
  • 1:1 dedicated trained critical care nursing staff
  • Crash carts with defibrillator & emergency resuscitation drugs
Advanced Surgical Facility

Ultra-Clean Modular Operation Theatre (OT)

Equipped to perform laparoscopic, general surgical, orthopaedic trauma, and gynaecological procedures adhering to strict infection control and sterile protocols.

  • High-definition HD Laparoscopic Surgical Tower
  • C-Arm Digital Fluoroscopy for Orthopaedic Fracture fixation
  • Advanced Anaesthesia Workstation & recovery bay
  • Electro-surgical cautery and vessel sealers
  • Autoclave & sterile processing department (CSSD)

In-House Diagnostic Centre

24×7 Pathology Lab, Digital X-Ray & Ultrasound

Accurate, rapid laboratory tests and diagnostic imaging facilities available round-the-clock for routine and emergency patients.

  • Complete Pathology: CBC, Blood Sugar, HbA1c, LFT, KFT, Lipid Profile, Thyroid, Dengue/Malaria NS1, Urine Analysis
  • Diagnostic Radiology: High-frequency Digital X-Ray (Chest, Bones, Abdomen)
  • Ultrasound (USG): Abdominal, Pelvic, Obstetric (Pregnancy scans) & KUB
  • Cardiac Diagnostics: 12-Lead Computerized ECG & Cardiac Enzymes (Troponin-I)
