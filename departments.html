Clinical Specialities & Surgical Services

Explore our wide range of multi-speciality departments, day-care procedures, surgical capabilities, and 24x7 trauma care.

General & Internal Medicine

Comprehensive Adult Healthcare, Chronic Disease & Fever Clinic

Our Internal Medicine department provides diagnosis and non-surgical management of complex diseases, lifestyle disorders, infectious diseases, and multi-system conditions.

Exact Treatments & Scope:

  • Diabetes Mellitus (Type 1 & 2)
  • Hypertension & Blood Pressure Control
  • Dengue, Typhoid, Malaria & Viral Fevers
  • Thyroid, Hormone & Lipid Disorders
  • Bronchitis, Asthma & Chest Infections
  • Gastritis, Acidity & Liver Management

General & Laparoscopic Surgery

Minimally Invasive Keyhole Procedures & Day-Care Surgeries

Performed in high-sterility Modular Operation Theatres equipped with advanced laparoscopic towers and C-Arm imaging for faster recovery and minimal pain.

Surgical Scope & Procedures:

  • Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy (Gallbladder Stone)
  • Laparoscopic Appendicectomy (Appendix)
  • Inguinal & Umbilical Hernia Mesh Repair
  • Piles, Fissure & Fistula Laser Surgery
  • Abscess Drainage & Cyst Excision
  • Diabetic Foot Ulcer & Wound Debridement

Orthopaedics, Joint Care & Trauma

Bone Fractures, Joint Arthroscopy & Spine Management

24x7 emergency fracture reduction, plaster casting, trauma surgical stabilization, and non-surgical arthritis management.

Orthopaedic Treatments:

  • Emergency Fracture Plaster & Casting
  • Fracture Surgery (Plating, Nailing, Screws)
  • Knee & Hip Osteoarthritis Management
  • Sciatica, Slip Disc & Chronic Back Pain
  • Ligament Sprain, Tendon & Sports Injury
  • Osteoporosis Bone Density & Vitamin D Therapy
24x7 Trauma Call

Gynaecology & Obstetrics

Complete Maternal Antenatal Care, Normal Delivery & Women's Health

Dedicated labour room, fetal monitoring, post-natal care, and advanced gynaecological treatment for women of all age groups.

Maternal & Gynae Services:

  • Antenatal Mother & Fetal Checkups (ANC)
  • Painless Normal Delivery & Caesarean (LSCS)
  • PCOD/PCOS & Irregular Periods Management
  • Uterine Fibroid & Ovarian Cyst Treatment
  • Infertility Counseling & Primary Workup
  • Menopause & Pap Smear Cervical Screening
Maternity Packages

Paediatrics & Neonatal Care

Newborn Care, Child Vaccinations & Seasonal Illnesses

Comprehensive healthcare for infants, children, and adolescents delivered by experienced child specialists.

Paediatric Services:

  • Complete National Immunization / Vaccination
  • Paediatric Fever, Pneumonia & Chest Infection
  • Newborn Jaundice (Phototherapy)
  • Childhood Asthma & Allergies
  • Growth, Height, Weight & Nutrition Assessment
  • Diarrhea, Vomiting & Dehydration Care

24×7 Emergency, Trauma & ICU Care

Immediate Life-Saving Critical Care & Resuscitation

Round-the-clock emergency medical officers, trained critical care nursing staff, high-dependency ICU beds with continuous vital monitoring and oxygen backup.

Emergency Scope & Scope of Care:

  • Acute Road Accident & Trauma Stabilization
  • Severe Chest Pain & Cardiac Emergency Triage
  • Respiratory Distress & Oxygen/Ventilator Support
  • Acute Poisoning, Drug Overdose & Bites
  • Diabetic Ketoacidosis & Shock Management
  • 24x7 Ambulance Dispatch with Oxygen
