Understanding Viral Fever, Dengue & Monsoon Illnesses: When to Visit OPD vs Emergency

Medical Review: Dr. S.K. Sharma, MD (Internal Medicine)
Published: September 2026 6 min read

Doctor Verified Healthcare Information

This clinical guide is authored by the Department of General & Internal Medicine at S.K Multi-Speciality Hospital to help families distinguish between mild seasonal fevers and critical conditions like Dengue or Typhoid.

Introduction: The Seasonal Fever Dilemma

Seasonal changes and monsoon transitions often bring a sudden spike in viral infections, influenza, dengue fever, and waterborne illnesses across Delhi-NCR. While many fevers resolve with basic symptomatic care and hydration, certain infections require timely blood tests (Complete Blood Count / Dengue NS1 / Widal) to avoid life-threatening complications like sudden platelet drops or hypovolemic shock.

Common Symptoms: Viral Fever vs Dengue

It is essential to observe how the fever behaves over the first 48 to 72 hours:

  • Standard Viral Fever: Mild to moderate fever (99°F - 102°F), running nose, mild sore throat, general weakness, resolving within 3 to 5 days.
  • Dengue Fever: Sudden onset of high fever (103°F - 104°F), intense headache, pain behind the eyes (retro-orbital pain), severe joint and muscle aches ("breakbone fever"), nausea, and skin rash around day 3-4.
  • Typhoid: Step-ladder rising fever, persistent stomach ache, loss of appetite, and coated tongue.

🚨 Red-Flag Warning Signs for Immediate 24x7 Emergency Care

Do NOT wait if the patient experiences: severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, bleeding from gums or nose, extreme lethargy/drowsiness, cold and clammy skin, or breathing difficulty. Head directly to S.K Hospital 24x7 Casualty (077039 64716).

Role of Platelet Monitoring & Diagnostic Lab Testing

In suspected dengue cases, self-medicating with over-the-counter painkillers like Ibuprofen or Aspirin must be strictly avoided as they can worsen bleeding tendencies. Only Paracetamol under medical guidance should be used for fever control.

At S.K Multi-Speciality Hospital, our fully automated in-house diagnostic laboratory provides:

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC): Rapid platelet count and Hematocrit (HCT) analysis in under 30 minutes.
  • Dengue NS1 Antigen & IgM/IgG Serology: Accurate identification of dengue virus from Day 1.
  • Liver Function Test (LFT) & Widal: Checking for secondary organ stress and typhoid complications.

Home Care & Hydration Protocol

  1. Adequate Fluids: Drink ORS, coconut water, clear soups, and clean boiled water (minimum 2.5 to 3 liters daily for adults).
  2. Rest: Strict physical rest is crucial to allow the immune system to combat viral replication.
  3. Do Not Self-Medicate: Never take antibiotics for viral illnesses without a doctor's prescription.
