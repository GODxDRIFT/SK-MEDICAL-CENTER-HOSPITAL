About S.K Multi-Speciality Hospital

Comprehensive, Caring & Compassionate Healthcare 24×7

S.K MULTI-SPECIALITY HOSPITAL 24×7 is a premier healthcare centre located on Shiv Vihar Road (near Bikaner Sweets), Vikas Nagar, Hastsal, Uttam Nagar, Delhi.

We provide round-the-clock emergency care, inpatient treatment, outpatient doctor consultations, advanced diagnostic pathology, day-care surgeries, and post-operative monitoring. Our compassionate team of qualified medical officers, visiting senior consultants, and trained nursing staff ensure high-quality patient care in a comfortable, sterile, and supportive environment.
