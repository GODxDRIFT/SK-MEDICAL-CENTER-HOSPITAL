General & Laparoscopic Surgery

Why Laparoscopic Keyhole Surgery is Safer & Faster: Gallstones, Appendix & Hernia Treatment

Medical Review: Consultant General & Laparoscopic Surgeon
Published: 2026 4 min read

Surgical Care Advisory

Authored by senior surgeons at S.K Multi-Speciality Hospital detailing minimal access keyhole procedures for abdominal and day-care surgical conditions.

1. What is Laparoscopic (Keyhole) Surgery?

Unlike conventional open surgery that requires a large 5 to 7-inch incision through the abdominal wall, Laparoscopic Surgery utilizes 3 to 4 tiny incisions measuring just 0.5 to 1 cm. A miniature high-definition camera (laparoscope) and specialized fine micro-instruments are inserted to perform the surgery with millimeter precision.

2. Key Advantages of Laparoscopy Over Open Surgery

  • Minimal Pain & Discomfort: Significantly reduced surgical trauma to surrounding abdominal muscles.
  • Minimal Blood Loss: High precision visualization prevents blood vessel damage.
  • Negligible Scarring: Tiny keyhole incisions heal with minimal cosmetic marks.
  • Same-Day or Next-Day Discharge: Most patients walk within 6-8 hours and go home within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Low Infection Risk: The internal organs are not exposed to open ambient air, drastically cutting infection rates.

3. Common Conditions Treated with Laparoscopy at S.K Hospital

  1. Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy: Complete removal of gallbladder harboring symptomatic stones or sludge.
  2. Laparoscopic Appendectomy: Immediate emergency removal of inflamed or infected appendix.
  3. Hernia Repair (Inguinal, Umbilical, Incisional): Mesh placement with minimal abdominal disruption.
  4. Laser / Minimal Access Piles & Fissure Treatment: Painless daycare ano-rectal procedures.
